What we do

Think fast. Argue well.

01 Weekly debates

Every session runs real BP debates: four teams, one motion, fifteen minutes to prepare. It's the quickest way to get better at thinking on your feet.

02 Coaching & feedback

Every round gets judged with detailed feedback. Experienced members run workshops on case building, rebuttal, extensions and adjudication.

03 Tournaments abroad

We send teams and judges to competitions across Europe and to the major championships, competing against universities from around the world.

The format

BP in 30 seconds

British Parliamentary is the format used at the World Universities Debating Championship. Two teams argue for the motion, two argue against it, and everyone competes for first place.

Beyond Helsinki

We compete internationally

Members represent Aalto at university tournaments ("IVs") around Europe and at championships such as the European Universities Debating Championship (EUDC) and the World Universities Debating Championship (WUDC).

  • Debate against students from dozens of countries
  • Travel as speakers or as adjudicators
  • Build skills in public speaking, critical thinking and teamwork
  • Meet a community that loves a good argument
